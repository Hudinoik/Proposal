# TCO Proposal Generator

A simple tool for creating client proposals for **The Contractors Office Inc (TCO)**.
It runs on your own computer, in your web browser. There is nothing to install, no
account to create, and it works without an internet connection.

---

## ⚠ Important legal note (please read)

The legal clauses that come with this tool (Limitation of Liability, Hold Harmless &
Indemnification, Annual Pricing Review, Term & Termination) are **example wording only**.
**They are NOT legal advice.** Before you send a real proposal to a client, have a
**qualified attorney review and approve every clause**, then paste the approved wording
into **Settings → Legal clauses**.

---

## How to open it

1. Find the file named **`index.html`** in this folder.
2. **Double-click it.** It opens in your web browser (Chrome or Edge work best).
3. That's it — you're ready to make a proposal.

> Tip: to keep it handy, right-click `index.html` → *Open with* → Chrome, and/or
> drag it onto your browser's bookmarks bar.

---

## Making a proposal (the everyday steps)

1. On the **New Proposal** tab, fill in the **proposal number/date** and the
   **client's details**.
2. Choose a **package** from the drop-down.
3. (Optional) Add a short **personal note** to the client.
4. Watch the live preview on the right update as you type.
5. Click **💾 Save as PDF**.
6. In the print box that appears, set the destination/printer to **“Save as PDF”**,
   then click **Save**. Email that PDF to your client.

---

## Setting it up for TCO (do this once)

Open the **Settings** tab to make it yours:

- **Company & branding** — your company name, contact details, and the person the
  proposal is “prepared by”.
- **Main colour / Heading colour** — click the colour squares to match TCO's brand.
- **Logo** — click *Choose file* and select your logo image (PNG or JPG). A smaller
  file works best. If you don't add a logo, your company name is shown as text.
- **Packages** — add, edit, or remove your pricing packages. Put **one included
  service per line**.
- **Intro & About / Pricing text** — edit the standard wording that appears on every
  proposal.
- **Legal clauses** — replace the example wording with your lawyer-approved text
  (see the legal note above).

Your changes save automatically **on this computer**.

---

## Don't lose your work: Backup

Because your settings are saved inside this browser, it's smart to keep a backup:

- **Settings → Download backup** saves a small file with all your packages, clauses,
  and branding.
- **Settings → Restore from file** loads that backup back in — also how you move the
  tool to a **new computer** (copy `index.html`, open it, then restore your backup).

---

## Frequently asked

**Do I need the internet?** No. Everything runs on your computer.

**Where are my proposals saved?** Each finished proposal is saved by you as a PDF when
you click *Save as PDF*. The tool remembers your *settings* and the *current* form, but
does not keep a library of past proposals.

**I changed computers / cleared my browser and my packages are gone.** Restore them from
your backup file (Settings → Restore from file). This is why the backup matters.

**Can I start over?** Settings → *Reset to example defaults* puts everything back to the
starting examples. Download a backup first if you might want your current setup again.
