TCO AGREEMENT GENERATOR - START HERE
=====================================

GET GOING (10 seconds)
  1. Right-click the ZIP you downloaded -> "Extract All..." and pick a
     permanent home for the folder (e.g. Documents\TCO Agreement Generator).
     Don't run it from inside the ZIP - extract first.
  2. Open the folder and double-click:  Create Desktop Shortcut.bat
     -> puts a "TCO Agreement Generator" icon (your TCO logo) on the Desktop.
  3. From now on, open the app from that Desktop icon. It opens in its own
     window, like a normal program. Works completely offline.

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html                    ... the app itself (all your data lives here*)
  TCO Agreement Generator.bat   ... opens the app in its own window
  Create Desktop Shortcut.bat   ... run once to get the Desktop icon
  tco.ico                       ... the TCO logo icon
  Start TCO AI Helper.bat       ... optional: one-click AI drafting on your
  TCO-AI-Helper.ps1                 Claude subscription (see README.md)
  tco_ai_helper.py              ... Mac version of the helper
  README.md                     ... full instructions

*Your packages, prices, wording and drafts are saved by your browser on this
 computer automatically. Use Settings -> "Download backup" now and then to
 keep a backup file (also how you move to a new computer).

AI DRAFTING WITH YOUR CLAUDE SUBSCRIPTION (optional, one-time setup)
  1. Install Claude Code from https://claude.ai/download and sign in once
     with your Claude account.
  2. Double-click "Start TCO AI Helper.bat" and leave its window open.
  3. In the app, paste meeting notes and click "Draft with Claude".

IMPORTANT
  Keep all these files together in the same folder. The legal wording is not
  legal advice - have your attorney review it before sending to clients.
