# Privacy Policy — TCO Agreement Generator

**Effective date: 19 July 2026**

TCO Agreement Generator (the "App") is published by TCO (The Contractors
Office). The App is a tool for preparing client service agreements.

## What the App collects: nothing

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
Everything you type — client details, fees, agreement wording, settings —
is stored only on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics,
no tracking, and no advertising.

## AI drafting (optional)

If you choose to use the optional AI drafting feature, the meeting notes
you paste or dictate are sent, at your request, to the AI provider you
have configured — Anthropic (Claude) or DeepSeek — to be read and turned
into a draft. That content is handled under the chosen provider's own
privacy policy. If you never use AI drafting, nothing ever leaves your
device.

## Voice notes (optional)

Voice notes are transcribed on your device using Apple's built-in speech
recognition. Audio is not sent to us — we never receive or store it.

## PDFs you create

Agreements you export as PDF are saved or shared only where you choose
to send them (Files, email, messaging apps, printers).
